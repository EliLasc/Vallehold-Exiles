The Duke of the Vallemhir territory

Kazis is a staunch opportunist and constantly seeks new sources of power. The greatest desire of Vallemhir is to overcome the king he serves under.

Ever since the king took to the throne, Kazis has been the butt of the king's policies. Whether it be sending the most soldiers to a warfront, providing the most relief supplies to an attacked territory, or constructing new royal facilities, Kazis is always the one assigned to it.
