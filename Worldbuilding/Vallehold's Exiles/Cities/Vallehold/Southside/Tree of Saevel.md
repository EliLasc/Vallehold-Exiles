A large tree at the highest point of the [[Valle Jungle]] that used to be the home of [[Saevel Fennorin]].

Gameplay elements
**
- Druids or magically adept sense a presence from the tree
- The tree has a hidden hole/entrance masked by magic
- Inside the tree is the old home of an elf
- This elf fled here long ago and started a colony
- This colony eventually became the natives of this island
- Before passing away, the elf left behind a guardian deity for their descendants.
- The guardian goes by the name Essos, and when addressed properly will assist you.
- If players finds the hidden tome of the elf, they learn of the guardians name and how to command it properly
**
