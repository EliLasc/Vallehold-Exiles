Old ruins that housed the ancient [[Saevelite|Saevelites]] in the [[Valle Jungle]].

Gameplay elements
**
- Players can get visions in this location to help with their future decisions
- Some relics can be found that can teach spells or provide bonuses
- 2 +1 items will be randomly generated within a hidden chest in this area
- A Tabaxi ghost lives here that can be spoken to and tells of a beast that protects the island and has been caged under the prison
