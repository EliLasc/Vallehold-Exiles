A ravine in the [[Valle jungle]] that connects to the [[Valle outpost]].

Gameplay elements
**
- Underground waterway that leads to the eastern military outpost
- Old scrawlings along the walls that depict native life and the power of water
- A path branches off the waterway and has a hideout spot for a prisoner NPC
- This NPC is hostile at first and attempts to attack the players if not spotted by them first
- If befriended, they will help the players escape the island
