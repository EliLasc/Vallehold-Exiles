Players start the session on a boat named [[Aubretia]] that is taking them to Vallehold Island, a prison island that they must escape

**
- Pre [[Aubretia]] Attacked (1 hour)
    
	- Players start shackled on the lower deck with an anti-magic crystal in the center of the room
	- Players are given the chance to introduce themselves in this first scene
	- After introductions, a guard will enter the Brig and give a rundown of how life on the island will be
	- Following this, they start handing out rations
	- At this point, players roll a perception check, with a high enough perception check they can hear a prisoner mutering “It's coming for us, It's coming for us, It's coming for us”
	- If questioned the prisoner says “the great one approaches, and we are its victims.”
	- Players can interact with the guard and try to steal from him as he passes by
	- Players can also talk to and interact with the other prisoners during this time
		- There are 3 other prisoners in the room with them
			- Human - [[Darius Torba]] (rogue) Male
				- Sentenced to Vallehold for breaking into a bakery
			- Dwarf - [[Hastel Grimgrip]] (fighter) Female
				- Sentenced to Vallehold for killing a tax collector while drunk
			- Kobold - [[Mop]] (sorcerer) ???
				- Sentenced to Vallehold for ecoterrorism (overpopulated a pond with tadpoles and ruined the local ecosystem)
	- When the guard reaches the final player, the muttering prisoner shouts “May the gods have mercy on our souls before the sea drowns them!”
	- The ship is then attacked by a massive creature

- [[Aubretia]] Attacked (2-3 hours)
    
	- The ship receives a massive attack
	- The DM rolls a d2 to determine the next side of the ship that will be attacked 
		- 1 = Port 
		- 2 = Starboard
	- Every 10 min (or allotment of time) the DM rolls another attack on the ship
	- Each attack can't miss and deals 6d10 damage
	- After each attach 1d4 sea creatures board the top deck of the ship and attack the crew
	- The captain steering the ship will make a DC 15 vehicle (water) check at disadvantage when the attack occurs
		- D20 + 6 (+3 dex, +3 vehicle (water) prof)
	- On a success, cut damage in half, and take 0 damage on a natural 20
	- The ship has 250 starting health, and if the ship is destroyed while the players are still on it, they are knocked unconscious and wake up on the coast with no items.
	- Each attack hits a random side of the ship, which will cause damage to the rowboats on the ship’s sides
		- Rowboats take 5 hits before destroyed
	- In between the attacks, players make decisions to best escape and grab supplies
	- Players can also attempt to rescue any of the prisoners and recruit them at their own risk
		- There are 5 named prisoners on the ship
		- 3 were introduced in the brig
		- 1 is in the kitchen
			- Halfling - [[Panwan Greentop]] (hunter)
				- Sentenced to Vallehold for hunting in the royal forest without permission
		- 1 is on the deck fixing the rigging
			- Half orc - [[Ulgan]] (Barbarian)
				- Sentenced to Vallehold for raiding villages
	- There are a couple magic items and useful supplies that can be taken from the ship if players are resourceful
