Campaign Summary

Whether you stole from a tavern, lead a revolution, tripped a tax collector, or were wrongly convicted, you are doomed to exile on Vallehold Island. The island is famous as a hunting ground for rare animals, military outpost, trade port for rare minerals, and as the home of Valle prison, your new place of residence.

All I need from you is a Lvl 1 character made in D&D 5.5e, why they were exiled, and why they did it.

The first session will be aboard the prisoner transport ship Aubretia on its way to the island.
